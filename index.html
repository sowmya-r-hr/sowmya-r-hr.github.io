Case study 01 · HR systems

Building an in-house candidate-tracking system when a commercial ATS wasn't in the budget

My roleProject lead & business owner
TeamMe + 1 developer
Users15, every day
StatusStill in use

The problem

Every sourced candidate lived in someone's Gmail inbox. Recruiters forwarded resumes by hand, there was no shared pipeline, and nobody could see what had already been submitted. Client submissions took a full day, and we were losing placements to faster competitors. A commercial ATS was out of budget.

The goal

Give every recruiter instant access to vetted candidates so we could submit to new clients the same day, without adding software spend.

What I did

1 · Built the caseTook a build-vs-buy cost comparison to the CEO, framed around the cost of doing nothing: lost deals and hours spent searching inboxes.
2 · Defined itWrote the requirements for two modules, Job Postings and Applicants, holding only vetted candidates to keep the data clean.
3 · Tested itPartnered with a developer and ran user acceptance testing on each module before release.
4 · Rolled it outTrained the team, ran weekly feedback meetings, and still own the roadmap as features are added.

Results

24h → 2htime to submit candidates to clients
3 → 1 daycompany-wide turnaround
15daily users
$0licence spend

Case study 02 · HR operations & compliance

Standardizing onboarding across states to stop errors reaching candidates and payroll

My roleProcess owner
ScopeAll account managers
RegionsCanada + US hires
Result~75% fewer form errors

The problem

Each account manager onboarded new hires their own way. Forms were scattered across inboxes and drives, so outdated, duplicate and wrong-state forms kept reaching candidates and payroll, creating rework and compliance risk.

What I did

  • Compiled current forms by state into one shared, version-controlled library.
  • Wrote one onboarding checklist and walked every account manager through it.
  • Supported HRIS testing and compliance checks so the process held up.
  • Prototyped an e-onboarding module; developers built the first version from it.

Results

~75%fewer duplicate or expired forms
1process for every account manager
Lesson: most compliance problems are process problems. Current forms, one checklist and clear communication stop errors before anyone has to fix them.
